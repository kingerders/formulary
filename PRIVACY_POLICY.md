# Privacy Policy — Formulary

**Last updated: February 25, 2026**

Formulary ("the App") is developed by Erdinç Erder ("we", "us", "our").  
This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Formulary.

---

## Information We Collect

**Formulary does NOT collect any personal information.** Specifically:

- No account required — the app works entirely without signing in.
- No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or identifiers are collected.
- No location data is collected.
- No camera or microphone access.
- No contacts access.

Formula content, favorites, and personal notes are stored entirely **on your device** using local storage (SwiftData on-device database). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

---

## In-App Purchases

Formulary offers optional in-app purchases (discipline unlocks) processed entirely through Apple's App Store / StoreKit 2. We do not handle or store any payment or billing information.  
Apple's Privacy Policy governs all purchase transactions: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

Purchase records are stored by Apple and synced via your iCloud account. We do not store them independently.

---

## Analytical Data

Formulary does **NOT** use any third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, etc.).

App Store Connect may provide Apple's standard aggregated analytics (crash reports, usage metrics from users who have opted in to share with developers via iOS settings). We cannot identify individual users from this data.

---

## Internet Usage

The App uses the internet **only** to render LaTeX formulas (the KaTeX library is loaded from the CDN: `cdn.jsdelivr.net`). Formula content itself is bundled locally and does not require internet access after first launch.

No user data is transmitted over this connection.

---

## Children's Privacy

Formulary is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal data from any user, there is no specific data handling concern for any age group.

---

## Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy |
|---|---|---|
| KaTeX (Khan Academy) | LaTeX formula rendering | [MIT License](https://khan.github.io/katex/) |

CDN endpoint: `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/katex@0.16.9/`

---

## Data Security

All app data (favorites, notes) is stored locally using SwiftData on your device and is protected by iOS's standard encryption and sandboxing mechanisms. We have no access to this data.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

---

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

- **Email:** erdinc@erder.dev
- **GitHub:** <https://github.com/kingerders/formulary>
